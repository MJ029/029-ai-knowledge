Computer vision extracts meaning from images and video — what's in the frame, where it is, and what's happening.

## Core tasks

- **Classification** — what's in this image.
- **Detection / segmentation** — where objects are, pixel-by-pixel or box-by-box.
- **Generation** — synthesizing new images from text or other images.

## Why it matters

Modern computer vision runs on the same [Deep Learning](#/topic/deep-learning) backbone as language models, and increasingly the two merge — see [Generative AI](#/topic/generative-ai) for text-to-image and multimodal models.
