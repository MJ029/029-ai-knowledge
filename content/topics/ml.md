Machine learning is the practice of building systems that improve at a task by learning from data, rather than following hand-written rules.

## Core idea

Give a model examples (inputs + outcomes), it finds patterns that generalize to new inputs. The three broad flavors:

- **Supervised learning** — learn from labeled examples (spam / not spam).
- **Unsupervised learning** — find structure with no labels (clustering, embeddings).
- **Reinforcement learning** — learn from reward signals over a sequence of actions.

## Why it matters

Most of the AI systems you interact with — recommendations, fraud detection, [Deep Learning](#/topic/deep-learning) models, [LLMs](#/topic/llms) — are machine learning at the core, scaled up.
