MLOps is the discipline of taking a model from a notebook to a system that runs reliably, at scale, and can be trusted.

## What it covers

- **Versioning** — data, models, and code, all tracked together.
- **CI/CD for models** — automated training, evaluation, and deployment pipelines.
- **Monitoring** — drift detection, latency, cost, and quality regressions in production.
- **Serving** — batch vs real-time inference, scaling under load.

## Why it matters

A model that works in a notebook is a prototype. MLOps is what turns it into something you can depend on — the same instinct that runs [Agents](#/topic/agents) in production.
