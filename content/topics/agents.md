Agents are [LLMs](#/topic/llms) wired up with tools, memory, and a loop — so instead of answering one prompt, they can plan, act, observe the result, and keep going until a goal is done.

## Core idea

- **Tool use** — the model can call functions/APIs (search, code execution, databases) instead of only generating text.
- **Planning loop** — think, act, observe, repeat, until the task is done or it gives up.
- **Memory** — short-term (conversation) and long-term (retrieved context, stored state).
- **Multi-agent systems** — specialized agents coordinating on a larger task.

## Why it matters

This is where LLMs stop being autocomplete and start being able to *do things* — which is powerful, and also why [MLOps](#/topic/mlops) discipline and [Ethics & Safety](#/topic/ethics-safety) guardrails matter more here than almost anywhere else.
