Ethics & Safety covers the risks, biases, and guardrails that come with building and deploying AI systems — the part that matters once a model leaves the notebook.

## Core concerns

- **Bias & fairness** — models can encode and amplify biases present in training data.
- **Alignment** — making sure a model's behavior actually matches what we intend, especially as it gets more capable.
- **Misuse** — generated content used for misinformation, fraud, or harm.
- **Autonomy risk** — [Agents](#/topic/agents) that act in the world raise the stakes beyond text generation.
- **Privacy & consent** — training data provenance, copyright, personal data.

## Why it matters

Every topic in this graph eventually runs into this one. [LLMs](#/topic/llms) and [Generative AI](#/topic/generative-ai) put these questions in front of the general public; building responsibly means treating this as core work, not an afterthought.
