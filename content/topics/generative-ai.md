Generative AI covers models that create new content — text, images, audio, video, code — instead of just classifying or predicting.

## Core idea

Learn the distribution of the training data well enough to sample new, plausible examples from it. The current dominant approaches:

- **Diffusion models** — for images/audio, denoise from random noise step by step.
- **Autoregressive transformers** — for text/code, predict the next token given everything before it (see [LLMs](#/topic/llms)).
- **GANs** — an older approach, generator vs. discriminator in competition.

## Why it matters

This is the wave that put AI in front of the general public — chat, image generation, coding assistants, [Agents](#/topic/agents). It also raises the sharpest [Ethics & Safety](#/topic/ethics-safety) questions: misuse, misinformation, copyright.
