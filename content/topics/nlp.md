Natural Language Processing is the field of teaching machines to work with human language — reading it, parsing it, generating it.

## Core tasks

- **Classification** — sentiment, topic, intent.
- **Sequence labeling** — named entity recognition, part-of-speech tagging.
- **Generation** — summarization, translation, dialogue.
- **Representation** — turning words/sentences into vectors (embeddings) that capture meaning.

## Why it matters

NLP used to mean hand-built grammars and statistical models. Today it's almost entirely driven by [Deep Learning](#/topic/deep-learning), and its frontier *is* [LLMs](#/topic/llms).
